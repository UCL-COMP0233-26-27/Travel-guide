# Iceland

We would love to visit Iceland because of its breathtaking natural scenery, including glaciers, waterfalls, volcanoes, and hot springs. We are especially excited to see the Northern Lights and relax in the Blue Lagoon. Iceland would be an amazing place to explore nature and experience something completely different.
