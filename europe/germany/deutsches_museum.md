## Deutsches Museum

I would like to visit Deutsches Museum because it is the largest science and technology museum in the world. I like to see how science developed in the past. For example, there is an original Z3—the world's first programmable digital computer. Also, I can experience the interactive physics exhibit. It perfectly combines my academic interests with the chance to explore a beautiful Bavarian city.

More info at [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsches_Museum) and [wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Munich)
