# Palawan

I’d like to visit Palawan in the Philippines for its limestone cliffs, clear lagoons and coral reefs. Exploring the islands around El Nido by boat and snorkelling would be an exciting way to experience its geography. The food also appeals to me, especially fresh seafood and local dishes such as lato, a seaweed salad. Palawan’s combination of scenery, outdoor activities and food makes it somewhere I’d love to explore.
