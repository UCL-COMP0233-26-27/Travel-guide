# Chengdu

We would love to visit Chengdu because it is a beautiful
city in China, famous for its giant pandas, delicious
Sichuan food, and rich cultural heritage.

We are especially interested in visiting the Chengdu
Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding, trying authentic
Sichuan cuisine, and exploring the city's historical sites.

More information at [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengdu)
and [Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Chengdu).
